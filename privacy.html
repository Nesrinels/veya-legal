Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026 · Version française

This policy explains what personal data the Veya app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can exercise your rights. Veya is a tool that helps sports coaches follow their clients remotely.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

The app is published by Nesrine Lounissi, hereafter "Veya", "we", "us".
Contact: veya.app.contact@gmail.com

2. Data we collect

Account data

  • Name, email address and password (the password is stored in encrypted form; we cannot read it).
  • Your role in the app: coach or client.

Training and well-being data (that you enter)

  • Completed workouts, sets, weights and comments.
  • Body weight, measurements and progress photos.
  • Weekly check-ins and coach replies.
  • Nutrition plans, meals and meals marked as eaten.
  • Appointments and notifications.

Coach-specific data

  • Private notes about their clients (visible only to the coach who wrote them).
  • Tracking of their clients' subscriptions and payments, entered manually for information only.
  • Information about their own Veya subscription (dates, amounts, declared payment method).
Sensitive data. Body weight, measurements, progress photos and nutrition plans relate to your health and physical condition. You provide them voluntarily and we handle them with particular care. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

What we do not collect: bank card numbers, clients' CCP or BaridiMob identifiers, your phone contacts, precise location. Payments are made outside the app.

3. Why we use this data

  • To create and secure your account and let you sign in.
  • To enable the coach–client relationship: programs, nutrition, tracking, check-ins, appointments.
  • To send you service messages: password reset code, notifications.
  • To manage coaches' subscriptions.
  • To maintain, secure and improve the app.

We do not use your data for advertising and we do not sell it.

4. Legal basis

  • Providing the service you requested (account, tracking, notifications).
  • Your consent, in particular for health and well-being data you choose to enter. You can withdraw it by deleting the relevant data or your account.
  • Our legitimate interest in securing the service and preventing abuse.

5. Who can see your data?

  • Your coach, if you are a client: they see your tracking data (workouts, weight, photos, check-ins, nutrition). A coach's clients cannot see each other's data.
  • Your clients, if you are a coach: they see the programs, plans and messages meant for them, but not your private notes.
  • Our technical providers, only to run the app:
    • Supabase: hosting, database, authentication and file storage (including photos).
    • An email delivery service, for reset codes and account-related messages.
  • Authorities, if required by law.

Some providers may store data on servers located outside Algeria. We choose providers that apply recognized security measures.

6. How long we keep your data

  • For as long as your account is active.
  • When you delete your account, your personal data is deleted or anonymized. Copies may remain for a short time in technical backups before being erased.
  • Some information may be kept longer if the law requires it.

7. Your rights

At any time, you can:

  • access your data and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate information;
  • delete your account and your data;
  • object to certain processing or withdraw your consent;
  • request the portability of your data.

To do so, use the Delete my account option in the app (Profile screen) or write to veya.app.contact@gmail.com. We reply within 30 days at most.

These rights are recognized in particular by Algerian law no. 18-07 on the protection of individuals in the processing of personal data. You may also contact your national data protection authority if you believe your rights are not respected.

8. Users outside Algeria and international transfers

Veya can be used from several countries. Wherever you are, this policy applies, and you also benefit from the rights granted by the law of your country. For example, if you live in the European Union or the European Economic Area, the GDPR gives you in particular the rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and portability, as well as the right to lodge a complaint with the data protection authority of your country.

Our providers may store and process your data on servers located in countries other than yours. When this is the case, we choose providers that apply recognized security and data protection safeguards.

9. Security

Communications with the app are encrypted. Access to data is limited by strict rules: a user can only access their own data and the data their coach or client is meant to share with them. No system is perfect, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Choose a unique password and do not share it.

10. Minimum age

Veya is reserved for people who are 18 or older, or the age of legal majority in their country if it is higher. Coaches must not invite minors. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you believe a minor has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. In case of a significant change, we will inform you in the app. The date of the last update is shown at the top of this page.

12. Contact

For any question: veya.app.contact@gmail.com
